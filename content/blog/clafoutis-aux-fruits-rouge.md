The **Clafoutis** is a timeless French classic, a delightfully custardy, baked dessert that strikes the perfect balance between a flan and a light cake.

While traditional clafoutis is made with black cherries, this dessert can be made with any fruit. For this recipe, I use a mix of red berries. But I've also made versions with apple, pear, or even lychee, which is perfect when they're in season in Mauritius.

![Une part de clafoutis](/images/clafoutis.webp "Une part de clafoutis !")

Here is a quick, foolproof recipe using almond flour for extra moisture and flavor, serving 6 people.

---

## Quick Info

- **Servings:** 6 persons
- **Prep time:** 15 minutes
- **Baking time:** 35 minutes
- **Oven temperature:** 180°C (350°F)

---

## Ingredients

- **50 g** all-purpose flour
- **50 g** almond flour
- **50 g** granulated sugar *(plus 1 tablespoon for sprinkling on top)*
- **20 g** butter *(melted, plus extra to grease the baking dish)*
- **5 g** salt (a generous pinch)
- **33 cl** milk (approx. 1 ⅓ cups)
- **4** large eggs
- **1 dash** vanilla extract
- **200 g** red fruits / berries (fresh or frozen raspberries, blueberries, blackberries, strawberries)
- *(Optional)* A dash of dark rum to taste

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Prep the Oven & Cake Tin

- Preheat your oven to **180°C** (350°F), static heat.
- Butter your cake tin or baking dish.
- Spread the red berries evenly across the bottom of the dish.

### Melt the Butter

- Place the **20 g of butter** in a microwave-safe ramekin and melt it for about **30 seconds** in the microwave. Set aside to cool slightly.

### Mix the Dry Ingredients

- In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the **flour**, **almond flour**, **sugar**, and **salt**.

### Incorporate the Eggs

- Crack the **4 eggs** into the dry mixture.
- Whisk thoroughly until smooth and completely incorporated.

### Add Butter and Milk

- Pour in the melted butter and whisk well.
- Gradually add the **33 cl of milk** little by little while continuously whisking to avoid lumps and obtain a smooth, crepe-like batter.

### Flavor to Taste

- Stir in a **dash of vanilla extract**.
- *(Optional)* Add a splash of rum if you like an extra aromatic touch.

### Bake

- Pour the batter over the red berries in the buttered dish.
- Bake at **180°C for 35 minutes**. The clafoutis is done when it is puffed up, set in the center, and lightly golden brown on top.

### The Finishing Touch

- Remove the clafoutis from the oven.
- Immediately sprinkle a **spoonful of sugar** over the warm surface for a delicate sweet crust.

---

## How to Serve

- **Temperature:** Delicious served warm straight from the baking dish or chilled from the fridge.
- **Pairing:** Serve with a generous scoop of **vanilla ice cream** or a dollop of whipped cream (*crème chantilly*).

*Bon appétit!*
